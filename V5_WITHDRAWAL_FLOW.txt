COUNCIL INVENTORY SYSTEM V5

NEW WITHDRAWAL FLOW
1. Select a supply and quantity.
2. Scan the required barcode(s).
3. Scanner closes and shows a Review / Confirm Withdrawal screen.
4. IMPORTANT: scanning alone does NOT deduct stock.
5. Review supply, quantity, current stock, remaining stock, and scanned barcode(s).
6. Click Confirm Withdrawal to send the withdrawal to the server.
7. Server validates the barcode(s), atomically marks itemized barcodes as WITHDRAWN, deducts stock, and records the transaction.
8. A Withdrawal Successful receipt appears with the remaining stock.
9. Done returns to the current page and refreshes the quantity.

If the server rejects the withdrawal, no stock is deducted and the user can scan again.

V5 also includes separated transaction-history tabs:
- All Activity
- Adding History
- Withdrawal History
- Other Changes

The Inventory Add Supply button continues to open the Add Supply page.
